✦ Why the Sunset Horizon in Emberglow Horizon Feels So Natural:

1. Worm's-Eye Perspective (PITCH = 114°, Horizon = 80%):
In typical sky domes, the sun is rendered high overhead with equal atmospheric scattering all around. But in real landscape photography (like your daisy prairie reference), the camera sits low in the grass looking across the rim of the earth. The horizon sits in the lower third (~80% down the screen).

2. Atmospheric Optical Banding:
At sunset, Rayleigh scattering turns the zenith deep navy (#0B1424), grading through slate blue (#1E537C), mauve (#A69AA8), and burning red-orange (#FF1C00 / #F7974F), with a blazing white-hot sun core (#FFE35A) right at the ground line.

3. Pollen AI Spectral Dispersion:
Notice how the flowers in Pollen AI have rainbow chromatic aberration on the petal tips. That same optical effect is simulated in Glimmer Vale through radial RGB offsets, giving that dreamy, glowing aura!
